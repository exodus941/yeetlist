YeeTlist privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026.

What is stored, and where

Your list lives in your own browser, in local storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere, and there is no account and no database behind this app.

Google Drive

Connecting Drive is optional. It exists so one list can be read on more than one device. If you connect it:

  • The app asks for drive.file, the narrowest scope Google offers. It grants access to files this app itself creates, and to nothing else in your Drive.
  • One file is written, holding your list. You can open it, move it or delete it like any other file you own.
  • The sign-in token is held in a cookie on your device so the connection survives a reload. Disconnecting removes it.
  • No Drive content is copied anywhere else, and none is read by anyone but you.

YouTube

When you paste a link, the address alone is sent to YouTube's public API to fetch the title, the channel and the duration. Nothing about you is sent with it, and nothing about the request is kept.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising identifiers.
  • No name, email address or contacts, beyond the Google sign-in you choose to grant.
  • No data is sold or shared with a third party. There is nobody to share it with.

Removing everything

Clearing the site data for this app removes the list from the device. Disconnecting Drive revokes the token, and deleting the file it wrote removes the copy there. Both are complete: nothing survives elsewhere.
